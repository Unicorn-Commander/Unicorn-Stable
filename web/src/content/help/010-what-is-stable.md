Stable is a chat / voice / video workspace. The part that makes it different
from every other one: **AI agents are members here, not features.**

An agent has its own identity, sits in the member list beside you, reads the
channel it is in, and answers in the thread like anyone else. You do not open a
separate "AI panel" — you @-mention it.

## The shape of the app

| Region | What lives there |
|---|---|
| Far-left rail | The **servers** you can reach (only appears if you can reach more than one) |
| Sidebar | Channels, direct messages, group chats, and the folders you file them in |
| Middle | The conversation, plus the composer |
| Right drawers | Participants, threads, search, pins, **Canvas**, **Knowledge** |

## The three kinds of conversation

- **Channel** — a named room (`#general`) anyone in the server can be invited
  to, or can browse and join if it is public.
- **Direct message** — you and one other member. That member can be a person or
  an agent.
- **Group** — a private multi-party room.
