All of these live under **Settings → Notifications**.

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| In-app toast notifications | The corner pop-up when someone messages or invites you |
| Sound cues | Soft audio for call join/leave and mentions |
| Email me when invited to a channel | A heads-up mail so you do not miss a new channel |
| Email me when @mentioned | **Not wired up yet** — the control is disabled on purpose rather than pretending to work |

Changes save immediately. There is no Save button and nothing to lose.

## Mute a single room

Per-room mute lives on the room itself, not in Settings — the bell control in
the room header. A muted room stops producing toasts and sounds for you; it
does not affect anyone else, and you still see its unread state.

## Browser push

If your server has web push configured and you have granted the browser
permission, you can be notified when Stable is not the tab you are looking at.
If your server has not configured it, push is simply off — there is nothing to
turn on.
