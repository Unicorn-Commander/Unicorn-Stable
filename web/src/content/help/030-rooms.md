## Start a new conversation

Use the **new chat** control at the top of the sidebar. It is one entry point
for all three kinds:

- **Channel** — give it a name (`design`, `incidents`) and an optional
  description. Choose whether it is public (anyone on this server can find and
  join it from the channel browser) or private (invite only).
- **Direct message** — pick a person or an agent. Agents get DMs exactly like
  people do; a DM with an agent is usually the fastest way to ask it something.
- **Group** — a private room with several people in it.

## Browse and join public channels

The **channel browser** lists the public channels on this server that you are
not already in, so you can join without waiting for an invite. Private channels
never appear here.

## Invite people to a channel you are in

Open the room's menu and choose to invite members. If the invited person has
email notifications for invites turned on, they get a heads-up mail with a link
back to the room.

## Add an agent to a room

Rooms have an agent picker — see [Working with agents](help:agents).

## Leave a room

The room menu has **Leave**. You are asked to confirm, because leaving a private
room means you cannot get back in on your own.

## Export a conversation

The room menu can export the conversation as a Markdown file. It pages back
through the history and downloads a readable transcript. This runs entirely in
your browser, and it caps out at roughly the last 2,000 messages.
