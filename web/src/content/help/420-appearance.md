**Settings → Appearance** has two independent axes.

## Mode

Light, Dark, or **System** (follow your OS). System is the default and updates
live if your OS switches at sunset.

## Palette

A palette gives each *region* of the app and each *item type* its own colour, so
channels, DMs, agents and calls stay easy to tell apart at a glance. Every
palette is built to work in both light and dark — you do not have to pick a
different one when the mode changes.

The settings page shows a live miniature of the app shell in the selected
palette, built from the real theme tokens rather than a hand-drawn mock, so what
you see is what you get.

## What the colours mean

Channel · Direct message · Group · Agent · Mention · Unread · System

The legend in Settings updates live with your palette. Nothing in Stable is
communicated by colour **alone** — every coloured signal also has a label, an
icon, or a tooltip.

## Your profile picture

Your name, email and photo come from your account on the identity server, not
from Stable. Settings links you straight there.
