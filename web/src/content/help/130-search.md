Four different tools, because they answer four different questions.

## "Take me to that room" — quick switcher

`Cmd/Ctrl K`. Type a few letters of a channel, person or agent and press
`Enter`. It also lists servers, and carries a **Browse servers** row.

## "Where was that message?" — global search

`Cmd/Ctrl ⇧ F`. Searches messages **across every channel** you are in on this
server. Results link straight to the message in context.

## "Where was that in *this* room?" — in-room search

The search control in the room header. Same idea, scoped to the room you are
looking at.

## "The important stuff in this room" — pins

Anyone in the room can pin a message; the pins panel is the room's shared list
of what matters. Pins are per-room and visible to everyone in it.

## "Things *I* want to come back to" — saved

**Save** on a message files it in your personal saved list. Saved is private to
you, and spans every room.
